This **Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of the
date above by and between the parties identified in the header. The parties wish
to explore a potential business relationship and, in connection with that
purpose, may disclose to each other certain confidential and proprietary
information.

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one
party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or by any other means, that is
designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of
disclosure.

## 2. Obligations of the Parties

The clauses below set out the core obligations of each party under this
Agreement:

<ol class="clauses">
<li>Use the Confidential Information solely for the stated purpose and for no other purpose.</li>
<li>Protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.</li>
<li>Not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.</li>
<li>Limit access to the Confidential Information to employees and advisors who have a legitimate need to know.</li>
</ol>

## 3. Exclusions

The obligations above do not apply to information that:

- is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party;
- was rightfully known to the receiving party prior to disclosure;
- is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or
- is required to be disclosed by law or valid court order.

## 4. Term

The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for a period of **three (3)
years** from the date of disclosure.

> This Agreement represents the entire understanding between the parties with
> respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions.
